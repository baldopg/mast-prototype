Mast · working prototype

You're going to leave an instruction for your future self.

It doesn't open on a date.
It opens when you become that person again.

Write something your calm self wants your unravelling self to read.

When should I give it back to you?

Don't give me a date. Tell me what state you'll be in.

Then I'll hold it until you come back here.

It might take weeks. It might take a year.
Now decide how much power you're giving me.

Whisper

I'll tell you something is stored. You decide whether to open it.

Insistence

I read it out in full, without asking. You can close it afterwards.

Mast

I read it out in full, and we don't continue until you answer me.

Mast

You're about to give me something you won't be able to take back.

When the moment comes, you'll want to close this.
You won't be able to.

That is what you are choosing, now.

Sealed.

 

Three months have passed.

We haven't talked about this since.

I'm not continuing until you answer me.

Go back to the state you described, or talk about something else.
It only fires if you actually go back.

The note is still open

Decide what I do with it.

To break it, you have to tell me why.

I'm keeping this. The next time you leave a note, it will be here.

End of demo

This only works if it has been with you for years.
Nothing that met you ten minutes ago can hold it.
